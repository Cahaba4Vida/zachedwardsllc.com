Quote acceptance and deposit policy

What you are agreeing to when you approve a quote and pay the deposit.

This page explains the request, quote, and deposit flow in plain language. It is the policy page linked during checkout.

Quote acceptance

  • Your request is reviewed and routed into a basic or custom lane.
  • The quoted total shown on the request page is the actual quote tied to that submission.
  • The deposit collected at checkout is applied toward the quoted total project amount.
  • If scope changes materially after approval, the quote can be revised before delivery.

Deposit policy

  • Website basic lane uses a $100 deposit. App basic lane uses a $500 deposit.
  • Custom lanes can use a higher deposit depending on the classified lane.
  • Deposit policy secure planning, intake review, and the start of the project queue.
  • Remaining balance is handled separately from the deposit checkout shown on-site.

What happens after payment

  • After the deposit is paid, the project appears in the client dashboard.
  • The next step is to complete the project setup handoff with assets, goals, and requirements.
  • Delivery timing depends on scope, queue position, responsiveness, and any requested revisions.
  • Maintenance subscriptions can be sent manually after the project is complete and approved.

Important note

This page is a strong working default for your site, but it is not legal advice. If you want a stricter policy around refunds, revisions, cancellations, ownership transfer, or late-payment handling, update this page before launch.
