How to think about pricing

The easiest mental model is not message count. It is how much real digital work the bot can chew through in a month.

Quick tasks are things like reading and summarizing one page or drafting a short reply. Standard tasks are things like reviewing a dashboard, a spreadsheet, or a few pages and giving useful next steps. Heavy tasks are deeper research, reasoning, coding, or more involved browser workflows.

Infrastructure like SMS, paid APIs, storage, or especially heavy web-search usage can add cost depending on the workflow.
