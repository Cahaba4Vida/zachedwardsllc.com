Founder bio

Zachary Edwards

Building intelligent systems, disciplined environments, and a life that proves change is possible.

“Changing the world by changing myself first.”

Zachary Edwards is building his life around one conviction: the best way to change the world is to first change yourself.

From an early age, he felt the tension between who he was and the systems around him. He was never drawn to outdated paths, hollow milestones, or doing things a certain way simply because that was how they had always been done. What has always mattered to him is growth that is real — growth that sharpens a person, changes their life, and leaves a lasting mark.

That mindset showed up early. In school, Zach often felt boxed in by systems that rewarded compliance more than vision. By the time he reached high school, he already knew the traditional path was not building him into the man he wanted to become. So when the opportunity came to graduate early, he took it. At 16, he graduated early by testing out of high school, stepped into work, and began building a future on his own terms. While others told him he would regret leaving early, he knew exactly what he was doing. He was not walking away from something meaningful. He was moving toward something more real.

What Zach has always respected is transformation. Not appearance. Not recognition. Not empty milestones. Real change. The kind that comes through pressure, discipline, faith, and action. That is part of why he has always been drawn to things that demand something deeper from a person and reveal what they are made of.

At 18, he served a mission in Alabama, and that season changed his life. Before his mission, he dealt with severe social anxiety. Through that experience, he learned that people are not trapped by who they have been. They can grow. They can heal. They can become more. His mission deepened his faith in God, gave him hope in people, and showed him that personal transformation is not just possible — it is powerful.

After returning home, Zach worked in sales with Fox Pest Control, where he learned how business really works and how much the people around you shape your standards, habits, and future. That season taught him more than sales. It taught him how environments form people, how momentum is created, and how much smarter life becomes when the right systems are in place.

That vision became the foundation for Zach Edwards LLC.

He started the company because he saw a gap that most businesses were not solving. Too many intelligent, capable people are stuck wasting time inside outdated systems, buried in unnecessary friction, and taught to work harder when they should be learning to work smarter. Zach believes people should be able to let their intelligence work for them. He believes better systems create better lives, and that success should be built through leverage, design, discipline, and clear thinking.

Today, he is committed to building himself at every level — mentally, physically, spiritually, and operationally. He is pursuing nationals in powerlifting and has already set the Idaho and Utah state bench press records for his weight class. He is growing his business, building automated workflows, and laying the foundation for a future centered around intelligent systems and environments designed to help people become their best.

At the center of it all is one belief: real impact begins with the person you choose to become. Zachary Edwards is here to build a life that proves change is possible, to challenge mediocrity, and to create spaces where others can rise higher too.

Changing the world by changing myself first.
